Opening

Converting global singletons into injectable services is trending from "best practice" to "hard lesson" in many SwiftUI codebases. Runtime surprises—missing environment values, duplicated ObservableObject owners, or cache/state mismatches—are exposing thin spots in how teams model ownership and lifecycle.

This Week's Big Story

Dependency Injection Patterns for SwiftUI Apps

Converting globals to injectables often surfaces runtime failures that only appear under specific navigation or device profiles. The core engineering problem is ownership: who creates, who owns, and who observes a service in a view hierarchy. Solve that and you remove a large class of intermittent bugs and test gaps.
