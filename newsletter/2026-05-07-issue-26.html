Opening

Sequential motion in SwiftUI starts simple and gets brittle fast: scattered withAnimation calls, shared observable flags, and concurrent state updates produce flicker, multi-render spikes, and views left mid-animation. This week I want to show a pragmatic way to own motion sequencing so your UI behaves under load and cancellation — not just on the simulator.

This Week's Big Story

PhaseAnimator Techniques for Smoother SwiftUI Animations

Phase-based coordination centralizes sequencing and interruption handling so animations don't fight each other. It reduces jitter under CPU pressure by making ordering explicit and measurable. If your screens depend on multi-step choreography, you'll want to think in phases, not isolated withAnimation calls.
