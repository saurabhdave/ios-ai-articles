Opening

SwiftUI apps are hitting that unglamorous phase where UX polish is blocked by rendering hitches that only appear on-device, in Release, under real scroll pressure. Meanwhile, Apple quietly shuffles programs (hello, Apple Upgrade) and folks on new M5 Pros are discovering bridged networking snags in VMs—both reminders that our tooling and platforms are moving under our feet while we ship. This week is about getting rigorous: traces over theories.

This Week's Big Story

Profiling SwiftUI Rendering Hitches in Instruments

SwiftUI hitches are rarely about one slow view; they’re about the main thread missing a frame deadline while recompositions, layout, and drawing accumulate. Guesswork won’t isolate a stall during a fast scroll. You need a trace that shows which views recomputed, why they did, and where the time actually went.
