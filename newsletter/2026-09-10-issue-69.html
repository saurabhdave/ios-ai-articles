This Week's Big Story

AsyncSequence Patterns for Real-Time SwiftUI Views

SwiftUI is fine with “fast enough,” but it falls apart with bursty, unordered, or long-lived inputs unless you own buffering, cancellation, and isolation. The core move is to treat each live source as an AsyncSequence boundary you can reason about and test, not as a delegate call spraying mutations across the app. If you’ve ever shipped a screen that only recovers after a full relaunch, this is the mental model that prevents it.
