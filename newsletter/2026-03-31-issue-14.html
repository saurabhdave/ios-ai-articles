This Week's Big Story

Speed Up Xcode Builds with Explicit Modules

Missing per-target module flags and implicit umbrella headers cause repeated Objective‑C header parsing between incremental builds, slowing developer feedback loops. Making modules explicit reduces reprocessing, trims CI time under certain workloads, and cuts down on transient linker/import errors that only show up incrementally. If your team cares about fast local iteration, this is the kind of maintenance that pays compound dividends.
