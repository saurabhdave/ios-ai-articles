Opening

Security headlines are loud, but most teams I talk to are quietly wrestling with build pipelines that still have “just this once” buttons. Between privacy audits, new Swift drops, and a TestFlight that’s grown up, our risk isn’t the tools—it’s the glue we write and the habits we codify. This week is about turning TestFlight from a file drop into a workflow that never surprises you.

This Week's Big Story

TestFlight 4.3 Workflow Upgrades for iOS Teams

Manual uploads, fuzzy groups, and unsymbolicated crashes are how otherwise solid teams lose days in pre-release. TestFlight 4.3 closes gaps, but only if you tighten your versioning, entitlements, and symbol handling in CI so the pipes carry the right bits every time. If you’ve ever shipped a hotfix that re-used a build number or watched a crash log turn to noise, you’ll want to see the wiring I recommend.
