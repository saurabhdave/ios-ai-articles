Opening

Dark mode stopped being a design nicety years ago — it's a runtime surface that can break accessibility, screenshots, and release confidence when tokens are poorly scoped. Teams are quietly wrestling with how to roll out palette changes safely without shipping visual regressions or forcing emergency rollbacks.

This Week's Big Story

Designing Color Schemes for Dark Mode in SwiftUI

A single mis-scoped semantic color can cascade into unreadable banners, rejected App Store screenshots, or huge re-render storms across a feed. This piece lays down pragmatic, production-tested rules for token scoping, runtime validation, and gated rollouts. Read it if you want rollout patterns that are reversible and auditable.
